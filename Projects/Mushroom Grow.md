# Mushroom Grow

Grow delicacy mushrooms, sell to high-end Austin restaurants.

## Where things stand
Diego already has a small grow tent set up + 2 grow bags. **Needs spores** — that's the only missing piece.

## The plan
- **#1 variety: pink oyster** — fruits at 75–95°F (loves Texas heat, no cooling ever), ~4 weeks to harvest, chefs pay $8–12/lb. Catch: 1–3 day shelf life → harvest morning, deliver same-day.
- **Rotation:** lion's mane ($8–12/lb) + blue oyster ($6–8/lb) for fall/winter/spring.
- **Buy liquid culture, not spore syringes:** North Spore $19.99 or Out-Grow $12; first order = 1× pink oyster LC + 1× lion's mane LC ≈ **$24–40**. One 10ml syringe does 1–2 bags.

## Economics
- 5-lb block → 2.5–3 lbs → ~$25–30 revenue vs ~$4.50–9 cost = **~$18–25 profit per block per ~6-week cycle**
- His 2 bags ≈ $50–72 first cycle; colonized bags = free spawn via grain-to-grain after that
- One restaurant at 5 lbs/week ≈ $200/mo; 3–4 accounts ≈ $600–800/mo from one tent
- Real constraint: consistency — stagger inoculations

## Texas rules
Cultivated whole mushrooms = raw produce → typically just a business license, no food establishment permit for restaurant sales. Cottage food law does NOT apply. $10–15 food handler cert = cheap insurance.

## Pitch targets (Austin)
Emmer & Rye, Odd Duck, Wink, Cannon + Belle, Garrison — walk in 2–4 PM with free samples, ask for the chef.
