# Viral Clips

Agent team that finds trending clips, adds a transformative angle, and posts across TikTok / Shorts / Reels for maximum exposure.

## The format (Diego's call)
**AI avatar overlaid on the clips** — an AI host reacting/commenting. Transformative only: straight reposts get suppressed/demonetized at account level.

## The shortcut: clipping campaigns
- **Vyro** (MrBeast network, ~$3 per 1,000 views) and **FindClout** hand out campaign-approved footage and pay per verified view — no follower minimum, no copyright risk
- ~$1–3 CPM vs Shorts ad revenue $0.03–0.08 RPM (~40–100x more per view early)

## Rules that matter
- Avatar overlay strengthens the transformative case **only if the script is real opinions/jokes** — generic AI filler ("wow, crazy moment!") gets flagged under the inauthentic-content policy
- **AI disclosure mandatory:** YouTube toggle, TikTok AI label (explicitly covers avatars), Meta "AI info" — labels don't reduce reach, skipping risks removal/YPP suspension
- Engagement pods, bought likes, auto-comment bots = banned

## Starter stack (~$35–45/mo)
One clipper (~$15/mo) + one avatar tool (Hedra $20/mo, HeyGen $29/mo, or D-ID $4.70/mo annual) + free CapCut.
