# AI Training Data Niche — Research & Ideas

Source: @remonbiz TikTok "22yo ML engineer shares key startups to watch" (2026-10-22). Diego asked for research to find new ideas in a similar niche.

## Verification (2026-10-22)
- **Luel** — REAL. Berkeley dropouts (William Namgyal + Inigo Lenderking), YC W2026, **$31.2M seed** (TikTok said "$3.5M" — the "31.2" got garbled), led by General Catalyst + Lightspeed. Rights-cleared multimodal training-data marketplace: labs submit a spec, Luel's contributor network films/collects it, QA'd, delivered in days. 1M+ submissions processed, 40+ active campaigns.
- **AfterQuery** — REAL. $3.2B valuation, YC's fastest-ever unicorn (18 months), founders 23/22. Pays ~100K verified pros (doctors, lawyers, engineers, finance) to produce expert reasoning data + RL environments. Hundreds of millions ARR, profitable. Clients: Nvidia, Thinking Machines Lab, Legora.
- **Clover (robot)** — UNVERIFIED. No trace of a "Clover" 5-person team with a $1,600 humanoid. The *trend* is real (Chinese humanoids under $6K, 1X Neo, Dyna Taku, Sunday Memo), but treat the specific company/price as unconfirmed.
- **GRU Space** — REAL. Galactic Resource Utilization Space, founder Skyler Chan (21/22, Berkeley), moon hotel 2032, deposits $250K–$1M. Not Diego's lane.

## The niche pattern
Frontier labs have consumed the public internet; synthetic data drifts. The scarce input is now **net-new human data with consent and provenance**: everyday-life video/audio (Luel) and expert reasoning (AfterQuery). NPR (Oct 2026) confirms the demand side: firms like Objectways pay thousands of people across India/Philippines/Nigeria to film themselves doing chores for robot training.

## Ideas for Diego (ranked)
Honest frame first: he can't out-Luel Luel ($31.2M) or out-AfterQuery AfterQuery ($3.2B). The play is the **niche wedge** — one data type, one vertical, where he's the domain insider.

1. **Spanglish conversational speech data.** Code-switched audio is scarce and voice-agent labs need it badly. He's literally producing Spanglish content (Aguacate). Collect and license natural Spanglish conversations — nobody owns this lane.
2. **Cannabis vertical expert data.** The AfterQuery playbook applied to a vertical he knows cold: budtender expertise, compliance reasoning, strain-effect knowledge as structured training data for cannabis retail AI assistants.
3. **Regional accent speech (Texas Spanish / Southern English).** Voice agents need accented real-world speech; generic datasets don't have it. He lives inside the source population.
4. **Skilled-trades egocentric video.** Real plumbers/HVAC/mechanics filmed doing real work — Luel's own catalog lists "egocentric craftsmanship video" as a premium category, and robotics companies are the buyers.
5. **Micro data-marketplace for one data type.** A tiny Luel for a single niche, run solo with agents doing collection orchestration and QA. Low capital, his agent skills are the moat.
6. **Niche RL environments.** AfterQuery's second product (environments capturing how pros work) shrunk to one profession — e.g., smoke-shop retail workflows, restaurant ops.

## Next step
