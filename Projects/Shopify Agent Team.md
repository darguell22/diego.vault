# Shopify Agent Team

A team of agents that research trending, high-performing products across the web and replicate them for Diego's Shopify store: find what's selling, source equivalents, execute listings and sales.

## The rule (Diego agreed)
Inspired-by / generic equivalents only — never branded knockoffs. Knockoffs = store shutdown risk.

## First research sweep (done 10/20)
Top 3 picks:
1. **Hydrocolloid acne patches** — ~179k units/30 days on Amazon US, landed $1–3, retail $10–15, ~70–80% margin, consumable = repeat buyers
2. **Magnetic glue-free eyelashes** — TikTok 16M impressions, 5.32% CVR, landed $1.50–3, retail $12–20, ~75% margin
3. **Generic 40oz insulated tumblers** — ~169k units/30 days, source $6–10, retail $25–40, ~60–70% margin, Q4 giftable (never marketed as "dupes")

Runner-up: motion-sensor under-cabinet LED lights.

Watch-outs: no disease/cure claims on beauty listings; heated apparel needs certified suppliers; confirm Q4 shipping timelines now.
